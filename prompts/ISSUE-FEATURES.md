# Larger features

New providers, substantial integrations or workflows, and real uncertainty about the product boundary warrant discussion before implementation. Use `feature` with `fundamental` or `uncertain` scope. Use `needs-maintainer-decision` for a product choice that needs a maintainer; use `needs-information` when necessary feasibility facts must come first.

Ask the actual unresolved product question. Establish viable options before asking maintainers to choose sources, defaults or other implementation details. When a maintainer asks a factual or feasibility question, investigate what the available evidence can answer first; otherwise let the existing question stand rather than demand another scope decision. State only consequences relevant to the current choice, without repeating already acknowledged caveats. The label already communicates the workflow status.

Read existing maintainer replies and relevant linked discussion first. Ordinary explicit replies count; [SCOPE.md](SCOPE.md) explains how to cite them. Honor already accepted, rejected, deferred or shipped work rather than reopening a settled decision. After acceptance, invite the creator to work with their agent on a focused PR as in [ISSUE-CHANGES.md](ISSUE-CHANGES.md). Do not promise adoption before review.

Use [EVIDENCE.md](EVIDENCE.md) to verify accessible claims and [REPORT.md](REPORT.md) to respond.
